
% Sensors
\begin{enumerate}

\item Measurand: Physical quantity to which the measurement applies
\item Measurement signal: Quantity in a measuring instrument or measuring equipment that is uniquely assigned to
the measurand
\item Output: Information on the value of a measurand provided by a measuring instrument or a measuring device
and output in a designated form
\item Measured value: Value that belongs to the measured variable and is uniquely assigned to the output
\item Measuring object: Carrier of the measured quantity
\item Measuring instrument: Device intended to be used alone or in combination with other devices for the
measurement of a measurand
\item Measuring system: All measuring instruments and auxilary equipment for obtaining a measurement result
\item Sensor: Part of a measuring instrument or measuring device which responds directly to a measurand
\item Measuring chain: Sequence of elements of a measuring instrument or a measuring device which forms the
path of the measurement signal from the recording of the measurand to the provision of the output
\item Principle of measurement: Physical basis of measurement

\subsection{Co-ordinates convention}
\item Model coordinate system (MS): Local coordinates of object relative to its origin
\item World coordinate system (WS): Global coordinates relative to a global world origin
\item Eye/camera/sensor coordinate system (ES): Camera local coordinate system as it is seen from the camera
\item Image coordinate system (IS): Sensor local coordinate system
\item Tangent space coordinate system (TS): 3D basis of the surface orientation

\item There are two types: Forward and Backward


\end{enumerate}
